To enhance your soccer team management website, consider adding the following features:

1. Player Statistics Dashboard: A comprehensive section where you can track individual player performance, including goals scored, assists, passes completed, and overall contribution to the team.

2. Team Schedule and Match Results: A dynamic calendar that displays upcoming games, opponents, and historical match results for easy reference.

3. Team Ranking and Standings: A live leaderboard that shows your team's position in the league, points earned, and points lost.

4. Player Recruitment and Transfer System: A feature that allows you to manage player transfers, negotiate deals, and keep track of your team's roster.

5. Fan Engagement and Community Section: A dedicated area for fans to interact, share their thoughts, and engage with your team. This could include forums, social media integration, and fan polls.

6. Training and Development Plans: A section for coaches to create and manage training schedules, drills, and strategies for improving team performance.

7. Match Analysis and Tactics: A tool for coaches to review game footage, analyze opponent strategies, and devise game plans.

8. Sponsorship and Merchandise Sales: A platform for team merchandise sales and sponsorship opportunities, helping to generate additional revenue.

9. Player Profile and Bio: A feature for players to create their profiles, share their personal stories, and connect with fans.

10. Real-time Notifications: Push notifications for important updates, such as player injuries, team news, and game alerts.
